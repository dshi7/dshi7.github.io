## Philosophy
- I am re-thinking how I think.
- AI makes entropy accumulate faster. I call it **the second law of thermodynamics** in software engineering. How to flight against AI slop is a new challenge.
- Keep sharp understandings on critical part.

## Practices

- Never use AI for the sake of using AI. Evaluating employees by token usage is as stupid as couting diff. 
- Agentic coding has a strong bias toward accretion: it’s good at adding code, but not nearly as good at removing complexity.
- Never send AI slop to co-workers without reading it. It hurts my decency.
- Don't be a junky diff cow. 
- Pay 90% of attention defining constraints, action space and boundaries where AI can play freely.
- Critical diffs (kernel optimization or compiler change) need very deep understandings. No parallelism. They are critical paths of my daily dev work.
