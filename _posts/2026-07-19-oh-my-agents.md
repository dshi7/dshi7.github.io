## Philosophy
- I am re-thinking how I think.
- AI makes entropy accumulate faster. I call it **the second law of thermodynamics in software engineering**. How to fight AI slop is a new challenge.
- Maintain a sharp understanding of the critical parts.
- Use agents to expand execution bandwidth, not to outsource understanding.
## Practices
- Agentic coding has a strong bias toward accretion: it’s good at adding code, but not nearly as good at removing complexity.
- Spend 90% of your attention defining the constraints, action space, and boundaries within which AI can play freely.
- Critical diffs (kernel optimizations or compiler changes in my case) require very deep understanding. No parallelism. They are the critical path of my daily development work.
- Never use AI for the sake of using AI. VPs evaluating employees by token usage are as stupid as those counting lines of diff. Maybe they’re the same people.
- Never send AI slop to co-workers without reading it first. It’s disrespectful to their time.
- Don’t become a diff-producing junkie.
