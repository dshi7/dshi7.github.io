## Topic

Mathematically the operator calculates `softmax(Q@K^T)@V` where online softmax is actually implemented based on a math trick.

- `D` for head dim (32\~128), `Z` for batch size (1\~8), `H` for head number (32\~128) and `N` (or N\_CTX) for sequence length (\~4K)

![FA params (Z/H/N)](/assets/img/posts/fa/FA params %28Z-H-N).png)

1-D tiling: `BM` for Q and `BN` for K/V. So, it's obvious that:
- q\_tile: BMxD
- k\_tile: DxBN
- qk\_tile/p\_tile: BMxBN
- v\_tile: BNxD
- output\_tile: BMxD

![FA tiling](/assets/img/posts/fa/FA tiling.png)

## Nvidia Blackwell FA

Warp specialization and its memory data path

![blackwell FA memory](/assets/img/posts/fa/blackwell_FA_memory.png)

- Values in SMEM buffers `q_tile[0]` and `q_tile[1]` stay persistant through the output loop iteration entirely and are wiped only when processing the next `Q` macro-tile.
- The `qk0/qk1` are data-independent spacial splits processed simultanenously on every `BN` step.
- When `qk/acc` consumes the currnet kv tiles (a pair of `BNxD` SMEM BUF), TMA engine loads the next pair to ring buffer.

## AMD MI350x FA

- No mbarrier, no TMEM and no warp specialization
- Async load, warp pipelining
- CU wavefront has VMEM pipe, LDS pipe, MFMA engine, VALU pipe and SALU pipe

To maximize throughput, a performant kernel is ping-ponging between MFMA and VALU.
