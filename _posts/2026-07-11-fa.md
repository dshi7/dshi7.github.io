## Topic

This note contains my first-principle understanding only. Not a complete technical kernel tutorial.

`softmax(Q@K^T)@V` where online softmax is actually implemented based on a math trick.

Denotions: `D` for head dim (32~128), `Z` for batch size (1~8), `H` for head number (32~128) and `N` (or N_CTX) for sequence length (~4K)

![FA params (Z/H/N)](/assets/img/posts/fa/FA params (Z-H-N).png)

1-D tiling: `BM` for Q and `BN` for K/V. And to confirm:
- q_tile: BMxD
- k_tile: DxBN
- qk_tile/p_tile: BMxBN
- v_tile: BNxD
- output_tile: BMxD
